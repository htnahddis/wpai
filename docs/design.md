**Primary typeface:** Helvetica Neue

**Weights selected:**

- 55 Roman – Primary typography.
- 65 Medium – Emphasis and stronger hierarchy.
- Light / Roman – Supporting text and lighter treatments.

Our intended application:

| Application             | Typeface weight |
| ----------------------- | --------------- |
| Main website headings   | Roman           |
| Body text               | Roman           |
| Supporting descriptions | Light / Roman   |
| Product names           | Roman / Medium  |
| Emphasised text         | Medium          |

# **3. Visual design language**

Our agreed direction was:

- Minimalistic, sophisticated, enterprise-oriented.
- Generous whitespace and clean editorial layouts.
- Asymmetrical compositions where appropriate.
- Thin outlines and subtle shadows.
- Curved connecting lines, dotted paths and geometric diagrams.
- Custom product icons with consistent geometry.
- Avoid generic SaaS feature cards and unnecessary decorative elements.
- Avoid overwhelming the website with animations. One primary motion concept per important section.


**The two most important decisions to preserve:**

1. **Colour:** `#1B1466`, `#FF00B7`, `#00B7FF` with white, black and soft neutrals.
2. **Typography:** Helvetica Neue, primarily 55 Roman and 65 Medium.

This is the design direction documented in our November 14 website blueprint, aligned with the earlier November 6 brand identity boards.
