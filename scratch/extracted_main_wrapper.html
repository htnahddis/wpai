Frequently asked questions

What is an AI agent builder?

An AI agent builder is a platform that lets you create AI agents, which can plan, decide, and take action on your behalf, all using plain language instead of code. On monday.com, you can start from a ready-made agent or build one from a prompt, then connect it to your tools and put it to work.

Can I build an AI agent without coding?

Yes. monday.com's AI agent builder is designed for people without programming experience. Describe what you want the agent to do, grant it access to the boards and tools it needs, and it's ready to run.

What is the difference between an AI agent and an automation?

An automation follows a fixed if-this-then-that rule. An AI agent reasons through a task, decides what to do next, and can handle exceptions along the way. monday agents combine both: they act autonomously while still following the guardrails and permissions you set.

Why is monday.com the best AI agent builder for teams?

monday agents work from the same context as the rest of your team's boards, docs, and workflows, so they act on real information rather than a generic template. Combined with scoped permissions, audit logs, and one-click connections to hundreds of tools, teams get agents that fit how they already work.

How do I connect monday agents to my other tools?

Connect with one click to tools like Salesforce, GitHub, and HubSpot, or reach any system through monday MCP and open APIs. Once connected, your agent has the context it needs to take real action, not just answer questions.

Can multiple monday agents work together?

Yes. Agents can hand off work to each other and to your team inside the same workspace, so a single request can move from research to draft to approval without anyone copying information between tools.

How much does monday AI agent builder cost?

AI agent capabilities are available on the Basic, Standard, and Pro plans, starting at $9/mo per seat.

Can you use monday agents inside the monday mobile app?

Yes, you can assign, review, and manage monday agents from the monday.com app on iOS and Android, so your agents stay accessible wherever your team works.
